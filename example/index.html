How it works

Three steps from goal to verified output.

Step 1

Define goal + constraints

Describe what you want to build. Set budget limits, allowed tools, and quality requirements.

Step 2

Orchestrator assigns to sub-agents

The leader agent breaks down work and delegates to specialized sub-agents. Progress is tracked automatically.

Step 3

QA verifies, human approves

QA agent validates outputs. Artifacts are produced, and you approve before anything ships.
